# Welcome to the BACHAM Beta Test! 🎉

Thank you for helping us test **BACHAM** — the 100% private, zero-bot AI Meeting & Lecture Assistant! 

Unlike other tools that send annoying bots into your meetings, BACHAM runs entirely on your local machine. It captures audio directly from your system or browser, meaning nobody else in the call will ever know it's running.

This guide will walk you through setting everything up and outline exactly what we need you to test.

---

## 🚀 1. How to Download & Install

Currently, we are testing the **Windows** version and the **Chrome Extension**. 

### Step A: Install the Desktop App (Windows)
1. Go to our official website: [bacham.vercel.app](https://bacham.vercel.app) (or your current deployed URL).
2. Scroll to the **Download Center** and click the **Download EXE** button under Windows.
3. Once downloaded, double-click `bacham-setup.exe` to install.
> [!NOTE]
> **Windows SmartScreen Warning:** Because our app is not yet officially published/signed, Windows will show a blue **"Windows protected your PC"** screen. **This is completely normal for new, unsigned apps!**
> 1. Click **"Advanced"** (small link text at the bottom of the warning).
> 2. Then click **"Run anyway"** to proceed with the installation.
> 
> Rest assured — BACHAM is 100% safe. We just haven't gone through Microsoft's paid code-signing process yet.

### Step B: Install the Chrome Extension
The Chrome Extension is highly recommended. It allows you to seamlessly record Google Meet, Zoom, and other browser-based calls.
1. Open Google Chrome, Brave, or Microsoft Edge.
2. Go to our Web Store link: [BACHAM on Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/bacham-%E2%80%94-ai-meeting-lectu/kfngjfenfpaladmjnogmihilchiednfl)
3. Click **"Add to Chrome"**.

## ⚙️ 2. Setting Up Your Account

1. Open the **BACHAM Desktop App**.
2. You will be greeted by the login screen. You can either:
   - Create a new account using your **Email and a Password**.
   - Click **"Sign in with Google"**. (This will instantly pop open a secure browser window for you to log in).
3. Follow the quick onboarding steps to choose where your recordings and transcripts will be stored locally on your hard drive.

## 🧪 3. What Features to Test (Your Mission!)

We want you to try breaking the app! Please test the following core workflows:

### A. Record a Meeting via Desktop
1. Open a YouTube video or start a test meeting (Zoom, Teams).
2. Open the BACHAM Desktop App.
3. Click the **Record** button to start capturing your system audio. 
4. Speak into your microphone and let the meeting audio play. 
5. Stop the recording and verify that the transcript generated correctly.

### B. Record a Meeting via Chrome Extension
1. Open a Google Meet call (you can just create an empty one: `meet.google.com/new`).
2. You should see a small BACHAM Floating Menu appear on the screen.
3. Click **"Start Capture"** on the floating menu.
4. Talk for a minute, then click **"Stop Capture"**.
5. Check your Desktop App — the recording should instantly sync via our secure local WebSocket connection!

### C. Test the AI Workspace & Notes
1. Open a finished meeting in the desktop app.
2. Play around with the **Notes Workspace**. 
3. Try generating an AI summary or asking the AI questions about the meeting.
